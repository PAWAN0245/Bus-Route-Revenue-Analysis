# Bus Revenue Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based bus revenue and route performance analysis dashboard. It analyses 1,000 bus departures across 10 routes using booking, ticket-price and competitor-price data.

The workbook brings together trip-level calculations and dashboard summaries to help review revenue, seat occupancy, route performance, demand levels and rule-based price recommendations.

> **Note:** This is a portfolio/practice project using the data contained in the workbook. The price recommendations are rule-based examples, not live pricing advice or proof of a production pricing system.

## Dashboard Preview

The `Dashboard` worksheet presents:

- Overall business KPIs
- Route-wise performance table
- Month-wise performance
- Day-of-week performance
- Monthly revenue chart
- Occupancy percentage by route chart
- Revenue by route chart
- Configurable high- and low-demand occupancy thresholds

## Workbook Structure

The Excel file contains three worksheets:

### 1. Dashboard

The dashboard summarises the trip data and displays:

| KPI | Workbook result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹21,959,110 |
| Seats Booked | 28,032 |
| Average Occupancy | 70.1% |
| Departures | 1,000 |
| Low-Demand Departures | 176 |

It also compares 10 routes using departures, available seats, booked seats, revenue, occupancy, average ticket price, average competitor price, price gap, low-demand departures and price recommendation.

### 2. Data

Contains 1,000 trip-level records across 10 routes. Each route has 100 departures in the dataset.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Bus Route | Bus journey route |
| Travel Date | Scheduled travel date |
| Total Seats | Total seats available for the departure |
| Booked Seats | Number of seats booked |
| Ticket Price (Rs) | Ticket price charged |
| Competitor Price (Rs) | Competitor price for the same route and date, retrieved from the competitor sheet |
| Revenue (Rs) | Revenue calculated from booked seats and ticket price |
| Day | Day of the week for the travel date |
| Occupancy % | Share of available seats that are booked |
| Demand | High, Medium or Low demand category based on occupancy thresholds |
| Price Gap (Rs) | Our ticket price minus competitor price |
| Price Action | Rule-based recommendation: Increase, Decrease or Hold |

### 3. Competitor_Prices

Contains competitor pricing records by bus route and travel date. A combined route-and-date key is used to match the competitor price to the corresponding trip in the `Data` worksheet.

## Business Rules and Calculations

### Revenue

Revenue is calculated for each departure:

```excel
=Booked Seats * Ticket Price
```

In the workbook, the row-level formula is:

```excel
=D2*E2
```

### Occupancy Percentage

Occupancy shows what percentage of available seats have been booked:

```excel
=Booked Seats / Total Seats
```

In the workbook:

```excel
=D2/C2
```

### Demand Classification

The dashboard uses editable thresholds:

- **High demand:** Occupancy is 80% or higher.
- **Medium demand:** Occupancy is 50% or higher but below 80%.
- **Low demand:** Occupancy is below 50%.

The thresholds are stored on the `Dashboard` worksheet and referenced by the formulas.

### Competitor Price Lookup

The `Data` worksheet retrieves the competitor price using `VLOOKUP` and a combined route-and-travel-date key:

```excel
=VLOOKUP(A2&"|"&B2,Competitor_Prices!$A$2:$D$1001,4,FALSE)
```

### Price Gap

The price gap compares our ticket price with the competitor's price:

```excel
=Ticket Price - Competitor Price
```

- Positive value: our ticket price is higher.
- Negative value: our ticket price is lower.
- Zero: both prices are equal.

### Price Recommendation

The workbook uses `IF` and `AND` conditions to generate a recommendation:

- **Increase:** Occupancy is at least 80% and our ticket price is less than or equal to the competitor's price.
- **Decrease:** Occupancy is below 50% and our ticket price is higher than the competitor's price.
- **Hold:** Neither of the above conditions is met.

The recommendation is generated by this rule-based formula in the `Data` worksheet:

```excel
=IF(AND(I2>=Dashboard!$K$23,E2<=F2),"Increase",IF(AND(I2<Dashboard!$K$24,E2>F2),"Decrease","Hold"))
```

These rules are simplified for this analysis. A real pricing decision may also consider seasonality, booking lead time, operating costs, remaining capacity and other market factors.

## Excel Skills Demonstrated

- `VLOOKUP` for competitor price matching
- `IF` and nested `IF` for demand classification and recommendations
- `AND` for testing multiple business conditions
- `COUNTIF` and `COUNTIFS` for counting departures and low-demand trips
- `SUM` and `SUMIFS` for revenue and route/month summaries

## Key Analysis Questions

This workbook can be used to answer questions such as:

1. What is the total revenue generated across all departures?
2. Which bus route generates the highest revenue?
3. Which routes have the highest and lowest occupancy?
4. How many departures are classified as low demand?
5. How does revenue change month by month?
6. Which days of the week have higher occupancy?
7. How does our average ticket price compare with competitor prices?
8. Which routes receive an Increase, Decrease or Hold recommendation?

## How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `Bus_Revenue_Analysis.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
3. Select the `Dashboard` worksheet to view the summary and charts.
4. Open `Data` to inspect the 1,000 trip records and calculated fields.
5. Open `Competitor_Prices` to review the competitor pricing data used by the lookup formula.
6. If you update the source data, make sure the formulas and dashboard ranges cover the updated rows.

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to organise operational data, compare competitor prices, calculate business KPIs and communicate findings through a dashboard. It was created as a learning and portfolio project focused on practical business and data analysis.
